06 — Risk

PAYDAY is a meme coin. It has no utility, no claim on any company's earnings, and no promise of value. Buy it only with money you are willing to lose entirely.

Payments are discretionary. They depend on creator fees actually accruing, and in any given cycle the amount sent out may be zero.
